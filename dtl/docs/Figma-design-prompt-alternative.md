# Figma Design Prompt – Alternative Auto Service (Distinct from DTL)

**Use this for a design that looks clearly different from the standard DTL-style template.** Different layout, typography, color system, and visual language.

---

## Design Direction: Industrial & Editorial

Avoid the "blue hero + cards + dark footer" pattern. Aim for: **raw, editorial, bold typography, asymmetric layouts, warm or monochrome palette.**

---

## Prompt for Figma

```
Create a website design for an auto service (car repair/mechanic) business. Use placeholder "[BRAND]". ONLY general mechanical services (no tyre, no car wash). 

**Critical: This must look clearly different from typical auto service templates.** No blue hero with dark overlay, no rounded card grids, no standard top-strip header. Aim for an industrial, editorial, or premium aesthetic.

## Design Direction – AVOID (typical auto site)
- Full-width hero with dark overlay and centered headline
- Blue primary, slate grays
- Rounded card grids
- Top contact strip + white nav bar
- Dark footer with link columns
- Symmetric, centered layouts

## Design Direction – USE INSTEAD

### Layout
- **Split-screen hero**: Image on one side (50%), headline + CTA on the other. Or: minimal hero with large typography, no full-bleed image.
- **Asymmetric sections**: Staggered grids, offset columns, content that breaks the center. Consider Bento-style blocks.
- **Editorial feel**: Big type, lots of whitespace, section dividers (lines or blocks) instead of cards.
- **Single-column narrative** on some pages instead of side-by-side.

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Strong serif (e.g. Fraunces, Playfair, Cormorant) OR condensed sans (e.g. Bebas Neue, Oswald) – NOT Inter/Geist.
- **Body**: Contrasting font – if headlines are serif, use clean sans for body.
- **Scale**: Go big. Hero headline 72px+ on desktop. Clear size hierarchy.

### Colors
- **Option A – Warm industrial**: Black, warm gray (#2d2d2d), cream/off-white (#faf8f5), accent orange or rust (#e07c3c).
- **Option B – Monochrome + accent**: Black/white, single accent (e.g. lime green #84cc16 or gold #ca8a04).
- **Option C – Dark mode**: Dark charcoal background, light text, one bright accent.
- **No blue/slate corporate palette.**

### Components
- **Sharp or subtle corners** (4px–8px), NOT pill-shaped or heavily rounded.
- **Borders over shadows** – thin rules, outlined sections.
- **Minimal decoration** – no icon blobs in colored circles unless very minimal.

### Header
- **Single-row, minimal**: Logo left, nav right. No top strip. Transparent or light.
- Or: **Overlay nav** – transparent on hero, solid on scroll.
- Thin bottom border, not a colored bar.

### Footer
- **Compact**: Logo, 1–2 link columns, contact. Or horizontal single row. NOT a large dark block with multiple columns.
- Light background option (cream/white) to contrast dark hero.

## Pages (Same Scope, Different Execution)

### 1. Homepage
- Hero: Split (image | text) OR typography-only with small supporting image.
- Services: Horizontal list or Bento grid, NOT 3 equal cards.
- Why us: Numbered list or bold statements, minimal icons.
- Contact: Inline or compact, not a big map block.

### 2. Services
- Editorial layout: Large title, body text, service list as table or simple list. Image as full-bleed strip or sidebar.

### 3. Booking
- Form: Clean, left-aligned labels, full-width inputs. No card wrapper – form floats on page.
- Or: Two-column (form | short info/steps).

### 4. Request Quote
- Same form style. Minimal.

### 5. Contact
- Split: Form left, map right. Or stacked. Avoid card-based contact blocks.

### 6. Gallery
- Masonry or irregular grid, NOT uniform 3-col grid. Or: horizontal scroll strip.

### 7. Testimonials
- Large quotes, author below. Horizontal scroll or full-width single testimonial. NOT card grid.

### 8. Header
- Minimal: [BRAND] | Home Services Book Quote Contact Gallery Testimonials
- Sticky, thin, border-bottom.

### 9. Footer
- One row or two. Links, contact, copyright. Light or dark but compact.

## Scope (Same as Template 1)
- General service only (revisions, brakes, suspension, engine, diagnostics)
- Booking, quote form, contact, gallery, testimonials
- NO tyre, NO car wash

## Responsive
- Mobile-first. On mobile: stacked layouts, full-width, nav as drawer.
- Preserve asymmetry where it works; simplify where it doesn't.
```

## Comparison: Template 1 vs Alternative

| Element | Template 1 (DTL-like) | Alternative |
|---------|------------------------|-------------|
| Hero | Full-bleed image, dark overlay | Split-screen or typography-first |
| Primary color | Blue | Orange/rust, lime, gold, or none |
| Typography | Sans-serif (Inter/Geist) | Serif or condensed |
| Cards | Rounded, shadowed | Borders, minimal, or none |
| Header | Top strip + nav bar | Single minimal row |
| Footer | Dark, multi-column | Compact, light or dark |
| Layout | Centered, symmetric | Asymmetric, editorial |

## When to Use Which

- **Template 1**: Client wants safe, corporate, familiar auto site.
- **Alternative**: Client wants something distinctive, memorable, or premium-feeling.
